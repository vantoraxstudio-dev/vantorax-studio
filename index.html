A Clear Process From Start to Launch

  1. Discovery

    Understand the client's business, services, target market, brand, requirements, and goals.

  2. Planning and Scope

    Agree on the website structure, pages, content, visual direction, functionality, project scope, and timeline.

  3. Design and Development

    Create the website using approved business information, branding, and authentic assets.

  4. Client Review and Revisions

    Give the client an opportunity to review the website and request changes. Revisions follow the agreed written scope.

  5. Launch and Handover

    Verify the agreed functionality, publish to the selected hosting environment, and provide the agreed handover information.

Timelines start once the required content, assets, and approvals have been received. Final deliverables, revision allowance, payment terms, and launch requirements are confirmed in writing before work begins.
